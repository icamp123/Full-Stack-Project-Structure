# Figma Wireframe
 
Complete the Figma wireframe and frontend design in this folder by Milestone 3.
 
## Milestone 2: setup only
 
All Milestone 2 asks for is that your group has a working Figma project and that everyone can get into it. You are not expected to design anything yet.
 
Create one Figma project for your group and paste its link below. One link for the whole team, not one per person.
 
**Figma project link:** https://www.figma.com/design/xfFHKgwsF7OyVmtUhFllmN/Untitled?node-id=0-1&t=evGP2PkyOqUpYixU-1
 
**Who on the team created the Figma project:** Vinnie Dinwiddie
 
Two things to get right now, because both are slow to fix later:
 
- Register with your **university email** at https://www.figma.com/education/ and apply for education access. A personal Gmail address will be rejected.
- Every member needs their own education verification. If you are invited to a project with edit access and have not verified within **seven days**, you lose edit access and the project becomes read only for you. That tends to happen right when Milestone 3 starts, so verify now.
A guide to setting up an account, teams, and projects: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hrHL2VLMl7g

The wireframe itself is due and graded in Milestone 3. See the Milestone 3 document for what it has to contain.
